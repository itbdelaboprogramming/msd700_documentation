# Live Camera

<RoleBadge role="user" />

The Live Camera view streams the robot's onboard video feed directly to your browser in real time, so you can see what it sees from anywhere.

## Opening the Live View

1. From the main menu (or from the Navigation screen), click the **camera icon**.
2. The video feed loads within a few seconds. A small indicator shows the connection quality.

## Using the Live View

- **Full screen**: Click the expand icon to fill your screen with the video for a clearer view.
- **Picture-in-picture**: Keep the camera open in a small window while working on the map, so you can watch and navigate at the same time.

## When to Use It

- Checking why the robot reports "Stuck" during navigation or coverage
- Confirming there's no obstacle, person, or hazard before starting a mission
- General remote monitoring of the space the robot is in

## Troubleshooting

**Video doesn't load / stays black**
: Check your internet connection first. If the problem continues, the robot may be offline: check its status in the unit list.

**Video is laggy or freezes**
: This is usually a network bandwidth issue between your browser and the robot. Try closing other bandwidth-heavy applications, or ask your administrator to check the connection.

**"Name resolution" or connection error message**
: This is a network configuration issue, not something you can fix from the dashboard: contact your administrator or technician.
